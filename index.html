About

Logic and imagination, applied to data.

I'm a computer engineering graduate focused on data, analytics and AI, and on using them to improve how real systems perform and decide.

I recently completed an Enterprise Analytics internship at Jio Platforms. I worked on an observability platform and learned how enterprise-scale systems are monitored, and how data supports reliability, performance and operational decisions.
